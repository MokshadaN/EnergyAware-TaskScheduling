How it works

Transparent by design

The simulator generates a reproducible workload and models heterogeneous virtual machines with different capacities and energy rates.

The energy-aware policy scores each placement using estimated energy use, current utilization, and available capacity. Compare it with least-utilized and round-robin policies.

Metrics are simulated comparisons, not measurements from a production data center. Use the controls to explore trade-offs, not to make operational decisions.
